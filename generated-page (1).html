Chennai Neighborhood Guide

Find the Right Chennai Pocket for Your Lifestyle

Chennai offers diverse residential environments. Compare top growth corridors and established central localities below.

OMR IT Expressway

High Rental Yield

Home to TIDEL, Ascendas, and ELCOT SEZ. High demand from tech professionals, great multi-screen theatres, international schools, and future Metro phase 2 connectivity.

Avg: ₹5,200 – ₹7,800 / sq.ft

ECR Coastal Belt

Scenic Luxury

Chennai's premier coastal highway from Thiruvanmiyur to Kovalam. Known for open sea air, low-density zoning, gated independent villas, and luxury weekend second homes.

Avg: ₹8,500 – ₹14,000 / sq.ft

Anna Nagar

Prime Central

Chennai's master-planned elite residential haven. Wide tree-shaded avenues, premium retail, metro stations, and established independent homes with high capital stability.

Avg: ₹12,000 – ₹18,000 / sq.ft

Adyar & South Coast

Elite Residential

Quiet, cultured residential pocket along the Adyar river basin and Besant Nagar beach. Close to prestigious clubs, Kalakshetra, and IIT Madras.

Avg: ₹14,000 – ₹22,000 / sq.ft

Velachery

Retail & Transit

Strategic bridge connecting South Chennai with airport transit, OMR, and Central city. Anchored by Phoenix Marketcity, MRTS railway, and family-friendly flats.

Avg: ₹6,500 – ₹9,200 / sq.ft

Medavakkam & Porur

High Growth & Plots

Rapidly appreciating suburban hubs. Outstanding value for residential plots, independent duplex homes, and gated layouts near DLF Cybercity & Sri Ramachandra.

Avg: ₹4,200 – ₹6,800 / sq.ft
